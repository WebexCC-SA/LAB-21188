

## Well done! You have successfully integrated Cisco Webex Contact Center with Salesforce Service Cloud Voice!


Additional Info:

- Salesforce queues and routing configurations are required for Omni-Channel to route VoiceCall records to agents and enable screen pop and full SCV functionality.

- Although Salesforce can route a VoiceCall record without executing the Omni Flow, no CRM logic is applied before the call reaches the agent. As a result, the call appears as a basic inbound/outbound VoiceCall record with no screen pop, contact matching, or business logic.

- Executing the Omni Flow is essential to apply CRM logic, perform customer matching, and ensure Salesforce routing decisions remain aligned with the telephony call path.

- For advanced configurations and customizations, refer to the [complete integration guide](https://app.tango.us/app/workflow/Integrate-Webex-Contact-Center-With-Salesforce--Service-Cloud-Voice--da86322c26ab41c4890989255e9661d7)
